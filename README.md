My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

Overview

Stranger Things is a science fiction horror series created by The Duffer Brothers.
It’s set in the 1980s in the small town of Hawkins, Indiana, where a group of kids discovers strange events connected to a secret government lab and a mysterious parallel world called the Upside Down.

Main Characters

Eleven (Millie Bobby Brown) – A girl with telekinetic powers

Mike Wheeler (Finn Wolfhard) – Brave and loyal friend of Eleven

Dustin Henderson (Gaten Matarazzo) – Funny and intelligent

Lucas Sinclair (Caleb McLaughlin) – Smart and cautious

Will Byers (Noah Schnapp) – The boy who disappears

My Favorite Quote

“Friends don’t lie.” — Eleven

Why I Love It

It mixes mystery, friendship, and supernatural adventure perfectly.

The 1980s vibe and soundtrack make it nostalgic and fun.

Each season keeps getting more exciting and emotional.

First Released:

July 15, 2016

⭐ Rating:

9/10 — It’s thrilling, emotional, and unforgettable!
